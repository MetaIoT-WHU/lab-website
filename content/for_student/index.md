## Prospective Students

We are always looking for self-motivated **PhD/Master/Undergraduate students** to join us. Our research topics include but are not limited to:

* **Wireless Sensing** & **Networking** (e.g., mmWave, LoRa, Backscatter)
* **Mobile Computing** & **AIoT**
* **Spatial Perception and Intelligence** for **Embodied AI**
* **Smart Health** & **HCI**


You can find more information about our research on the [HOMEPAGE](../) and the [PUBLICATION](../publication/index.md) page. If you are interested in the fields above and would like to consider working with us, please send your CV and transcripts to [Prof. Wei Wang](mailto:wangw@whu.edu.cn).

## Requirements

### For Graduate Applicants
Applicants are expected to be pursuing or hold a bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Communication Engineering, Mathematics or other related STEM majors.

* Strong background in mathematics and programming (C/C++, Python, MATLAB).
* Good command of English (reading and writing).
* Strong passion for the above research topics and desire to solve challenging research problems.
* Self-driven and responsible.

Our research often involves interdisciplinary knowledge, so having experience or prior knowledge in areas below are considered a plus (but not required):
* Proficient in deep learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow).
* Hardware design and development (e.g., FPGA, SDR).
* Wireless communications and signal processing.
* Embedded systems and IoT platforms.
* Robotics (e.g., Experience with ROS is a plus).
  
We do not expect applicants to be proficient in all these areas, but a strong willingness and ability to learn new skills is essential.


### For Internship Applicants
We welcome applications from undergraduate and graduate students for internships. During the internship, students will have the opportunity to work on real research projects under the guidance of our faculty and senior students. Interns are expected to contribute to ongoing research and may have the chance to co-author publications based on their work. Certain internships may also lead to full-time research positions after graduation.

Below are the basic requirements for internship applicants:
* Currently enrolled as a student (undergraduate preferred).
* Basic programming skills.
* Self-driven and responsible.
  
We highly encourage applicants doing internships offline at our lab in WHU. However, for exceptional candidates who are unable to join us in person, we may consider remote internships on a case-by-case basis.

## What We Offer
We provide a stimulating research environment with opportunities to work on cutting-edge projects. Students will have access to state-of-the-art facilities and resources, as well as opportunities to publish in top-tier conferences and journals. We also encourage collaboration with industry partners and participation in international conferences.

The lab offers **sufficient hardware** and **computing resources** to support research activities. By now, we have several high-performance servers equipped with GPUs for deep learning tasks (*e.g., NVIDIA H200, L40S, RTX 4090*), as well as various IoT devices (*e.g., USRP X310*) and sensors for experimental research. We are also building extendable testbeds for embodied AI (e.g., mobile robots, robot arms, LiDAR, mmWave radar) and wireless networking research.

For graduate students, we provide **competitive stipends** and **research funding** to support their academic pursuits. The destination of our graduates includes **key universities** (*e.g., WHU, WHUT, ZZU*), **leading tech companies** (e.g., Huawei, Tencent, ByteDance, Alibaba, DJI) as well as **key state-own companies** (*e.g., 南方电网, 航天科工*). For more detail about our alumni, please reffer ro the [TEAM](../team/#Alumni) page. 

For internship students, we highly value the contributions of our interns and often consider them for future full-time positions upon exceptional performance. Additionally, reference letters could be provided based on their performance during the internship. We have a strong track record of recommending our interns to top universities worldwide for further studies (*e.g., CMU, ETH, Waterloo, HKUST, HKU, CUHK*).


## Life at MetaIoT

We provide a vibrant and supportive environment for research. We encourage collaboration and open communication among lab members. Regular group meetings, seminars, and social events are organized to foster a sense of community and facilitate knowledge sharing. Sports activities are also encouraged to maintain a healthy work-life balance, with special lab funding for sports activities. Team building events are organized periodically to strengthen the bond among lab members. You can expect a dynamic and engaging research experience at MetaIoT Lab. More photos about life at our lab can be found at the [GALLERY](../gallery/) page.
